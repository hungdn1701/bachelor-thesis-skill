# School profile: PTIT (undergraduate engineering thesis)

**Status: community proposal, not a regulation of the Institute.** No institute-wide format rule for undergraduate theses was found when this profile was written. A rule from the student's faculty or advisor overrides anything here. State this basis in every report that uses this profile.

## Page and text

| Item | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Paper | A4, one-sided | VNU |
| Margins | Top 2.5 cm, bottom 3 cm, left 3 cm, right 2 cm | VNU (PTIT-VT1 uses 2.5 cm at the bottom) |
| Font | Times New Roman 13 | VNU, PTIT-VT1 |
| Line spacing | Multiple 1.3 | VNU (PTIT-VT1 allows 1.15–1.3) |
| Paragraphs | 6 pt between paragraphs, first line indented 1 cm | VNU |
| Page numbers | Bottom center; i, ii, iii before the Introduction; 1, 2, 3 from the Introduction | VNU, PTIT-VT1 |

## Order of parts

1. Cover, then inner title page (PTIT-VT1, PRACTICE)
2. Signed assignment sheet, if the faculty requires it (PTIT-VT1)
3. Advisor and reviewer evaluation pages (PRACTICE)
4. Abstract, about 360 words, at most 4 keywords (VNU)
5. Declaration, signed (VNU)
6. Acknowledgements (PRACTICE)
7. Work division, for a group thesis (PTIT-VT1, PRACTICE)
8. Table of contents, lists of figures and tables (PTIT-VT1, PRACTICE)
9. Symbols and abbreviations, in alphabetical order (VNU, PTIT-VT1)
10. Introduction, chapters, conclusion (VNU)
11. References, then appendices (PRACTICE; VNU places appendices first)

## Numbering and captions

- Chapters and sections use Arabic numerals: `Chương 1`, `1.1`, `1.1.1` (VNU).
- Figures and tables are numbered within each chapter: `Hình 1.1`, `Bảng 1.1` (VNU).
- Table captions go above the table; figure captions go below the figure (VNU).
- Equations are numbered within each chapter, in parentheses at the right margin (PROPOSED).
- References use IEEE style, numbered by first citation, with `[n]` in the text (PROPOSED; VNU sorts alphabetically by language).

## Cover

Institute name and faculty in capitals, PTIT logo, `ĐỒ ÁN TỐT NGHIỆP ĐẠI HỌC`, `ĐỀ TÀI:` with the title in capitals, advisor, student, student ID, class, cohort, program, then place and year, inside a frame (PRACTICE). The logo is available on the [Institute's website](https://ptit.edu.vn/) and remains its property; it is not covered by this repository's licenses.

## Language

For a thesis written in Vietnamese in a high-quality program, add an English title page and an English abstract (VNU). For a thesis written in English, ask the faculty whether a Vietnamese title page and abstract are required.

## Length

Reference values only: VNU asks for at least 50–75 pages for a graduation project and 35–50 pages for a graduation thesis, excluding figures, tables, references and appendices. PTIT-VT1 asks group projects for at least 70 pages with 2 students and 90 pages with 3.
