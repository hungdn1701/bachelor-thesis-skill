# School profile: generic

**Status: defaults, not a rule of any school.** Use this when the student has no school rule, template or defended thesis at hand, and ask them to request one from the faculty. Every report must say that format was not checked against a school rule, and must never state that the thesis complies.

Conservative suggestions the student may follow until a rule is found:

- A4, a serif font at 12–13 pt, wider left margin for binding, numbered pages.
- Parts in this order: cover, abstract, declaration, contents, abbreviations, introduction, chapters, conclusion, references, appendices.
- Chapters and sections numbered with Arabic numerals; figures and tables numbered within each chapter.
- Table captions above, figure captions below.
- One reference style used consistently, with every source cited and every citation listed.
